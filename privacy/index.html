Website policy

Information collected

When you request an estimate or contact MGD Skid Loader Services, you may provide your name, phone number, email address, property or project location, project details, photos and other information you choose to submit.

How information is used

MGD uses submitted information to respond to inquiries, discuss or prepare estimates, communicate about requested services, maintain business records, improve the website and protect the business from misuse.

CRM and service providers

The website uses a third-party CRM form provider to receive and manage inquiries. Information submitted through the form may be processed by that provider on MGD's behalf. Hosting, security, analytics or similar providers may also process technical website data.

Technical information

Website and hosting systems may collect information such as IP address, browser type, device information, referring pages and website activity. If additional analytics or advertising tools are added, this policy should be reviewed and updated as needed.

Sharing

MGD does not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers that support business operations, when needed to complete requested work, or when required by law.

Your choices

You may contact MGD to ask about information you previously submitted or to request correction or deletion where appropriate and legally permitted.
